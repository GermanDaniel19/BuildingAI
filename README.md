Real-Time Traffic Bottleneck & Incident Detector

Building AI course project

Summary

An AI-powered traffic monitoring solution designed to analyze live CCTV video feeds, detect incidents and early-stage bottlenecks, and automatically notify transit authorities before major gridlocks occur.

Background

Urban traffic management relies heavily on reactive measures—officers or systems usually respond after a severe traffic jam has already formed. 

* **Problem:** Manual CCTV monitoring by operators is inefficient, leading to delayed responses to accidents or stalled vehicles.
* **Impact:** Increased urban congestion, higher carbon emissions, emergency response delays, and hours lost in traffic daily.
* **Motivation:** Automating video feed analysis using AI allows city authorities to detect micro-anomalies early and intervene before traffic comes to a complete halt.

## How is it used?

The system monitors live CCTV camera streams positioned across critical city junctions and expressways.

1. **Continuous Video Processing:** Live RTSP video feeds from traffic cameras are continuously fed into the system.
2. **Automated Incident Alert:** When an anomaly (e.g., a stalled vehicle in a lane, sudden drop in speed, or lane block) is detected, an alert is generated.
3. **Dispatch Notification:** Traffic control centers receive instant alerts featuring the exact GPS location, live camera frame, and incident classification to send field units or adjust traffic signal timing.
